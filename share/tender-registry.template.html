Where this comes from

Every row was read from the state's own e-procurement portal — the public "tenders by closing date" listing, no login and no captcha involved. This is an aggregated working view, not an official government service. Confirm on the source portal before bidding: the tender document, its corrigenda and the closing time there are what count. Click a state to open its portal, then search the tender ID shown under each title — the portals issue per-session links that cannot be bookmarked, so a direct link would break.

Coverage is 35 of 36 states and union territories. Karnataka is absent because its tender search sits behind a captcha. Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep publish through the central CPPP portal, and Ladakh through the Jammu & Kashmir one.

Contract value is shown where the portal publishes it — Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Gujarat, Bihar and Chhattisgarh do; the NIC portals used by the other states do not.
